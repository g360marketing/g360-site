Frequently asked questions

Questions every small business asks before getting started.

How much does G360's service cost?

The investment varies based on the channels you choose (Google Ads, Meta Ads, website, SEO, or Google Business Profile) and your media budget. In the free diagnosis, we put together a proposal with clear pricing before any commitment.

Do I need to already have a website to hire you?

No. If you don't have a site yet, we build one from scratch, lightweight and designed to turn visits into inquiries. If you already have one, we assess whether it's worth adjusting the current one or building a new one.

How soon will I see results?

Ads (Google Ads and Meta Ads) usually generate the first inquiries within the first few weeks. SEO and Google Business Profile are more gradual — organic gains typically become more consistent from the second or third month on.

Do you work with any type of local business?

We work mainly with small businesses in Ocean, Monmouth, and Middlesex County, NJ — contractors (siding, roofing, plumbing, HVAC), shops, salons, clinics, service providers, and similar. In the free diagnosis, we assess whether your segment is a good fit for what we offer.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. You can cancel at any time, with no long-term lock-in. We're confident in the results our work brings, so we'd rather earn your business every month than tie you down with a contract.

Do I need to understand digital marketing to keep up?

No. Reports are written in plain language, with no technical jargon, so you can understand exactly where your investment is going month after month.
